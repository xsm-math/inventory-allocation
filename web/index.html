Evaluation metrics

Measurement and assumptions

Economic value is sales contribution less shortage penalties. Contribution deducts shipping and channel holding costs. Penalties encode preference weights rather than accounting expenditures. Fill rate is aggregate sales divided by aggregate demand.

Minimum allocations constrain quantities relative to forecasts; they do not guarantee realized service levels. The demand multiplier applies only to evaluation scenarios. Undispatched central stock has zero modeled holding cost.

Fixed seeds; 400 planning scenarios and 2,000 independent test scenarios. Intervals quantify conditional Monte Carlo uncertainty. The model covers one warehouse and one period.
